Hi! I'm **Jisu Kim**, a Master's student in the **HCI Lab at Ewha Womans University**, advised by **Prof. Uran Oh**.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Accessibility, Artificial Intelligence, and Augmented and Extended Reality (AR/XR)**. I explore how interactive technologies can empower people while preserving their autonomy.

I believe technology should expand what people can do, rather than take over the experiences, decisions, or enjoyable parts of a process. **Preserving user agency** is central to how I approach human–AI interaction and accessible technology design.

My current research focuses particularly on smart glasses and XR experiences for blind and low-vision people.
